Bio & CV

I'm Francesco, a UX designer based in Turin. I've spent five years at Arduino — now part of Qualcomm — going from intern to the reference designer of Arduino Cloud, designing across desktop, mobile and the tools that sit next to the hardware.

I like complex ecosystems: products where hardware, software and a community of makers meet, and where a small design team has to make choices that scale. Lately that has meant bringing AI into the workflow — prototypes built in hours with Bolt and Claude Code, so decisions get tested instead of debated.

Before product design I studied digital communication design at IAAD, and I still enjoy teaching: for a year I was a UI teaching assistant at Boolean, reviewing student work and running critiques.
